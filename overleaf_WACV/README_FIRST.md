## ⚠ The paper is deliberately over length

WACV allows **8 pages excluding references**. This draft is roughly **10–11 pages**:
6,167 words, **13 tables, 6 figures**. That is per your request to include everything so
you can choose. You need to cut roughly 3 pages.
