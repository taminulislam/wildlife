\subsection{Fate of the missed animals}

\Cref{sec:analysis} reports that of the 83 animals in the held-out videos, 9 are never
detected, 27 are found but rejected by the confirmation rule, and 47 are covered by an
accepted track. \Cref{tab:missed} gives the per-group statistics behind that split: missed
animals are smaller and shorter-lived than counted ones, and the rejected group sits between
the other two on every measure --- which is precisely why a threshold on any single one of
them cannot separate it.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Outcome & $n$ & Median box & GT frames & Cand.\ conf \\
\midrule
Counted & 47 & 38.1\,px & 102 & 0.76 \\
\textbf{Rejected by the rule} & \textbf{27} & 28.0\,px & 47 & 0.52 \\
Never detected & 9 & 19.9\,px & 47 & --- \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{\textbf{Fate of the 83 unseen animals.} The detector finds 74 of 83; the
confirmation rule then discards 27 of them. Missed animals are smaller and shorter-lived
than counted ones.}
\label{tab:missed}
\end{table}
